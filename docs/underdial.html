Under Dial — premium watches, under $1,000

A native iOS app for people who love watches but do not want to overspend. Discover pieces that suit your wrist and your style, run a WatchFit check before you buy, and grow a collection without the redundant fifth black-dial diver. Built and operated by Timberline Ventures LLC.

What Under Dial is

Under Dial is a discovery and buying advisor for watches under $1,000. Tell it what you already own and how you actually wear a watch, and it points you to pieces worth your money: the right case size for your wrist, a movement you can trust, and an honest nudge when there is a cheaper watch with the same specs. No hype, no $10k grail bait—just smart picks in a range real people buy.

For partners & advertisers

Under Dial reaches an engaged audience of watch buyers—people researching and purchasing microbrand and accessible-luxury watches, straps, and tools. For watch brands and retailers interested in partnering or affiliate placement, reach the studio at hello@timberlineventuresllc.com.
