Percolate — your coffee companion

A native iOS app for coffee lovers: discover roasters and single-origin beans, dial in your brew, log every cup, and find the next bag worth buying. Built and operated by Timberline Ventures LLC.

What Percolate is

Percolate helps everyday coffee drinkers and home enthusiasts get more from every cup. Browse and save roasters and beans, record brew recipes and tasting notes, and build a personal log of what you have tried and what to reach for next—then link out to buy the beans and gear you love.

For partners & advertisers

Percolate reaches an engaged audience of coffee buyers—people actively choosing beans, brewing equipment, subscriptions, and accessories. For coffee roasters, equipment brands, and subscription services interested in partnering or affiliate placement, reach the studio at hello@timberlineventuresllc.com.
