The engine underneath

It does not just suggest. It learns you.

Most apps hand you a list and hope something sticks. Ours watch what you actually keep, rate, and reach for, then work out the pattern behind it.

The bottles you cellar, the watches you save, the roasts you rate again. Each choice teaches the engine a little more about your taste, so the next recommendation is one level better than a list. It is closer to a friend who already knows what you go for.

And it travels. What Pour Picks learns about you gives Percolate a head start, and Cabin a sense of how you like to fly before you ask. One taste, read across the whole family.

How we work

Small studio, long memory

We pick niches we can serve well and stay in them for years. That patience is what lets the taste engine get good.

▲We build fewer things

We ship fewer features and fewer pages than we could, and we sweat the ones we keep. Depth is what earns a spot on someone's home screen, and it is what search rewards too.

◆Trust comes first

Our finance sites deal with people's money, so accuracy is not optional. Real sources, clear disclosure, and both languages from day one. The apps follow the same rule: your taste data is yours.

●No investors, no agenda

We are bootstrapped. Nobody is pushing us to chase a metric that does not serve the people using our stuff, so we can say things plainly and build for the long haul.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Timberline Ventures LLC?

Timberline Ventures LLC is an independent digital product studio. We build a family of consumer iOS apps that learn what you like, and we publish three bilingual financial guidance sites for ITIN holders in the U.S.

What do you mean by a taste engine?

Every Timberline app shares one engine that learns your taste from how you use it. The whiskey you keep, the watches you save, the coffee you rate. It does not just list options. It learns the pattern behind your choices, and what it learns in one app carries into the next.

What apps does Timberline Ventures publish?

Pour Picks for bourbon and whiskey, Stick Picks for cigars, Perfume Picks for fragrance, Percolate for specialty coffee, and Underdial for watches. Cabin, for private jet charter, is on the way. All are native iOS apps.

Who operates the ITIN information sites?

itinlending.net, itincreditcard.com, and itincreditscore.com are owned and operated by Timberline Ventures LLC. They publish independent, bilingual guides for ITIN holders. Timberline is not a lender or a broker.

Is Timberline Ventures a lender or financial advisor?

No. The ITIN sites are independent publishers, not lenders, brokers, or financial advisors. Everything on them is for information only.

How can I reach you?

Email the studio at hello@timberlineventuresllc.com. For help with a specific app, use the support link inside that app.
