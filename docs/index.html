How we work

The Timberline approach

Small studio, high standards. We pick niches we can serve well and stay in them for years.

▲Quality over quantity

We publish fewer pages and build fewer features than we could\u2014but every piece earns its place. Depth beats breadth in both search and App Store rankings.

◆Trust as product

Our finance sites are in the YMYL category where trust is non-negotiable. E-E-A-T signals, accurate citations, advertiser disclosure, and bilingual access are baked in from day one.

●Independent & bootstrapped

No outside investors, no agenda beyond serving users well. That independence lets us say things exactly as they are and iterate on what users actually need.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Timberline Ventures LLC?

Timberline Ventures LLC is an independent digital product studio that builds and operates a portfolio of consumer iOS apps and high-trust information websites, including three bilingual ITIN financial guidance sites and three native iOS collector apps.

Who operates the ITIN information sites?

The three ITIN sites—itinlending.net, itincreditcard.com, and itincreditscore.com—are owned and operated by Timberline Ventures LLC. They publish independent, bilingual (English and Spanish) guides for ITIN holders in the U.S. Timberline is not a lender or broker.

What apps does Timberline Ventures publish?

Timberline publishes Pour Picks (bourbon and whiskey cellar for iOS), Stick Picks (cigar collection journal for iOS), and Perfume Picks (fragrance wardrobe for iOS).

How can I contact Timberline Ventures?

You can reach the studio at hello@timberlineventuresllc.com. For support on a specific product, see that product’s support page.

Is Timberline Ventures a lender or financial advisor?

No. The ITIN information sites are independent editorial publishers—not lenders, brokers, or financial advisors. All content is for informational purposes only.
