About Timberline Ventures

An independent studio building iOS apps that learn your taste, and honest financial guides for people the big sites skip over.

What we do

Timberline Ventures LLC is a bootstrapped studio. We design and run a small set of products: native iOS apps for people who collect and care about things, and bilingual financial guides for ITIN holders. We go after niches where good software and honest information are both hard to find.

Every product we run is built for a specific audience and looked after with real care. When we make money, we do it in ways that line up with what the people using our products actually want, never at their expense.

One engine, many apps

What ties the apps together is a single taste engine. It learns what you like from how you use an app: the bottles you keep, the watches you save, the roasts you rate again. It does not just hand you a list. It works out the pattern behind your choices, so each suggestion is one step better than the last.

And what it learns travels. The taste Pour Picks picks up on gives Percolate a head start, and gives Cabin a feel for how you like to fly before you ask. That shared memory is the whole point of building these under one roof instead of as six unrelated apps.

The studio

Bob Guillow started Timberline Ventures in 2023. We keep it small on purpose. We move carefully, ship when things are ready, and take the long view on both product quality and search. No investors, no shortcuts.

We are based in the United States and work fully remotely.

Our portfolio

Financial information

  • ITIN Lending : Plain-English, bilingual guides to loans, mortgages, and auto and business financing for ITIN holders in the U.S.
  • ITIN Credit Card : How ITIN holders qualify for a credit card and build credit with it, no Social Security number required.
  • ITIN Credit Score : Building, checking, and improving a U.S. credit score on an ITIN, explained step by step in English and Spanish.

Consumer apps

  • Pour Picks : A bourbon and whiskey cellar for iOS. Catalog your bottles, track what they’re worth, and journal every pour.
  • Stick Picks : A cigar journal for iOS. Manage your humidor, keep an eye on collection value, and rate every stick you smoke.
  • Perfume Picks : A fragrance wardrobe for iOS. Organize your bottles, log your wears, and figure out what to reach for next.
  • Percolate : A specialty-coffee shelf for iOS. Track your bags, log cupping notes, and find the next roast worth buying.
  • Underdial : A watch advisor for iOS that gives a straight answer. Find your next solid watch under $1,000.
  • Cabin : Private jet charter for iOS. It learns how you like to fly, then points you to the few charters worth your time.

Editorial independence

The ITIN sites are independent publishers. We are not a lender, a mortgage broker, a financial advisor, or a credit repair company. When we point to a third-party product, we say so and disclose the affiliate relationship right on the page. Our editorial calls are not for sale.
