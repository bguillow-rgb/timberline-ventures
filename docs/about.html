About Timberline Ventures

An independent digital product studio building useful, trustworthy products for underserved audiences.

What we do

Timberline Ventures LLC is a bootstrapped studio that designs, builds, and operates a small portfolio of digital products\u2014native iOS collector apps and bilingual ITIN financial information sites. We focus on niches where good, honest information and well-crafted software are genuinely scarce.

Every property we operate is built for a specific audience, maintained with editorial care, and monetized in ways that align with what users actually need\u2014never at their expense.

The studio

Founded by Bob Guillow in 2023, Timberline Ventures is intentionally small. We move carefully, publish when things are ready, and take the long view on both search authority and product quality. No investors, no shortcuts.

We are headquartered in the United States and operate fully remotely.

Our portfolio

Financial information

  • ITIN Lending — Plain-English, bilingual guides to loans, mortgages, auto and business financing for ITIN holders in the U.S.
  • ITIN Credit Card — How ITIN holders qualify for, choose and build credit with credit cards — without a Social Security number.
  • ITIN Credit Score — Building, checking and improving a U.S. credit score on an ITIN, explained step by step in English and Spanish.

Consumer apps

  • Pour Picks — A native iOS cellar for bourbon and whiskey collectors — catalog bottles, track value, and journal every pour.
  • Stick Picks — The cigar collector’s journal for iOS — manage your humidor, track collection value and rate every stick.
  • Perfume Picks — A fragrance wardrobe for iOS — organize your collection, log wears and discover what to reach for next.
  • Percolate — A specialty-coffee shelf for iOS — build your coffee shelf, log structured cupping notes, and find your next bag.
  • Underdial — Watch discovery and an honest advisor for iOS — find your next affordable premium watch under $1,000.
  • Cabin — Private jet charter for iOS — it learns how you like to fly, then points you to the few charters worth your time.

Editorial independence

The ITIN information sites are independent publishers\u2014not lenders, mortgage brokers, financial advisors, or credit repair companies. Where we recommend third-party products, we disclose affiliate relationships clearly on each page. Our editorial positions are not for sale.
