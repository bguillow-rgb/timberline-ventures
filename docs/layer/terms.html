Last updated: 2026-08-02

Not medical advice

Layer offers general information about skincare products and common ingredient interactions. It is not medical advice, and it is no substitute for a dermatologist or doctor. Ingredient guidance is based on published research about ingredients in general, not on your skin. Patch test new products, and talk to a professional about anything involving a prescription, a skin condition, or pregnancy.

Subscriptions

Layer Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple account. It renews at the listed price until you cancel, which you can do anytime in your Apple account settings under Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

The retailer, not Layer, is responsible for the products they sell, their prices, shipping, and returns. Commissions never change which products the advisor recommends.
