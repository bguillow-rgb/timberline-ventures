The idea

Most charter apps hand you a search box and ten thousand tail numbers.

Cabin starts with you, then shows the short list.

No membership to join. No quote to chase before you know what you want. Tell Cabin the trip and it returns a small, considered set of aircraft and operators that suit how you fly, with a clean path to book the one you choose.

Why it gets you

It already knows what you like.

Cabin runs on the taste engine behind the rest of our apps. It learns your pattern: the classic cabins over the new ones, the operators with the safety record you want, the short hop to the coast more than the long haul. Every trip you take makes the next short list shorter and surer.
